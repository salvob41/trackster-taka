Trackster Taka is an unofficial companion for the Netrunner LCG. Inspired by the Magic: the Gathering life trackers that have been around for years, only purpose-built for everything Netrunner needs you to track.


TWO PLAY MODES

▸ BOTH MODE — Corp and Runner on one device
Two side-by-side panels for face-to-face play on a single phone. Each side has its own clicks, a split-tap credit counter (left half subtracts, right half adds), faction-specific stat chips, and a +click button for extra-click effects like Bankhar or Beth Kilrain-Chang. The flip button rotates a panel 180° so the player across the table reads it right-side up. An agenda ladder tracks both scores. The first side to reach the win target wins. The target is 7 by default, and you can set it from 1 to 10.

▸ SOLO MODE — track only your side
Playing across the table from a real opponent? Pick one side at setup. Enter the opponent's agenda points on the ladder. The OPP chip in the header tracks their tags or bad publicity. Side-rail stat chips (tags, core damage, hand size, MU, link, bad publicity) expand on tap to keep the less-used counters out of the way.


WHAT IT TRACKS

• Credits — Corp and Runner pools
• Clicks — 3 for the Corp and 4 for the Runner, tap to spend, refilled when the turn ends
• Agenda points — a ladder for each side, up to the win target
• Tags
• Core damage
• Bad publicity
• Memory units (MU)
• Hand size
• Link


ADAPTS TO YOUR DEVICE

• Portrait stacks Corp on top of Runner, with each side's agenda ladder on the right edge
• Landscape puts the two side by side, with the agenda ladder between them
• Rotate any time during a match — the layout adapts


QUALITY OF LIFE

• Game log records every action (clicks, credits, draws, agendas, turn flips) with round number and side
• Dice and marks — roll a d2, d3, d6, or d20, or pick a random HQ, R&D, or Archives mark
• Full-screen win overlay when either side reaches the win target
• Screen stays awake during a match — no auto-lock interrupting play
• Neon noir theme with glowing panels and a grid backdrop


PRIVACY FIRST

• No accounts, no sign-ups
• No internet — works completely offline
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Zero data collected, transmitted, or stored
