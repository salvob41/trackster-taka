Trackster Taka is an unofficial companion for the Netrunner LCG. Inspired by the Magic: the Gathering life trackers that have been around for years, only purpose-built for everything Netrunner needs you to track.


TWO PLAY MODES

▸ BOTH MODE — Corp and Runner on one device
Two side-by-side panels for face-to-face play on a single phone. Each side has its own clicks, a split-tap credit counter (left half subtracts, right half adds), faction-specific stat chips, and a +click button for extra-click effects like Bankhar or Beth Kilrain-Chang. The flip button rotates a panel 180° so the player across the table reads it right-side up. A shared agenda bar runs between the two as a tug-of-war — first to 7 wins.

▸ SOLO MODE — track only your side
Playing across the table from a real opponent? Pick one side at setup. The opposing score is entered via the OPP chip in the header so the agenda bar still works. Side-rail stat chips (tags, core damage, hand size, MU, link, bad publicity) expand on tap to keep the less-used counters out of the way.


WHAT IT TRACKS

• Credits — Corp and Runner pools
• Clicks — 1 to 4 per turn, tap to spend, long-press to refresh
• Agenda points — 7-segment victory ladder for each side
• Tags
• Core damage
• Bad publicity
• Memory units (MU)
• Hand size
• Link


ADAPTS TO YOUR DEVICE

• Portrait stacks Corp on top of Runner with a horizontal agenda bar between them
• Landscape splits the two side-by-side with a vertical agenda bar centered
• Rotate any time during a match — the layout adapts


QUALITY OF LIFE

• Game log records every action (clicks, credits, draws, agendas, turn flips) with round number and side
• Full-screen win overlay when either side reaches 7 agenda points
• Screen stays awake during a match — no auto-lock interrupting play
• Dark cyberpunk theme


PRIVACY FIRST

• No accounts, no sign-ups
• No internet — works completely offline
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Zero data collected, transmitted, or stored
